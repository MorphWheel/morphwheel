Features

Originals stay untouched

Every drop is a copy. Morphwheel never moves, overwrites or deletes a source file, and checks it after each conversion.

Works offline

Your images never leave your PC. No uploads, no account, no telemetry.

One-click undo

Changed your mind? Undo in the notification sends the new file to the Recycle Bin.

Whole selections

Drop many files at once. Formats that can't take them are dimmed, and the wheel says why.

iPhone photos too

HEIC and HEIF open after an optional one-time download, right from Settings.

At home in Windows

Follows your light or dark theme, accent color, High Contrast and animation settings.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Morphwheel is free and open source under the MIT license, with no trial, no account and no ads.

Does it upload my files?

No. Conversions run on your PC and nothing is sent anywhere. The only network request is the optional HEIC download, and only if you choose to install it.

Which Windows versions does it run on?

Windows 11, 64-bit. Windows 10 22H2 is best-effort. The installer needs no administrator rights.

Can it convert HEIC photos from an iPhone?

Yes, after a one-time optional download of the HEIC engine from Settings. It isn't bundled because its codecs are GPL-licensed.

Can I use a key other than Shift?

Yes. The trigger can be Shift, Ctrl or Ctrl+Shift, and the wheel comes in three sizes.

Can I add more formats?

Yes, with plugins written in Python. They appear in the wheel's More slice. The plugin guide has a complete converter in about thirty lines.
