# Code of conduct

Morphwheel is a community project. We want it to be a friendly, welcoming place for everyone,
whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude that someone meant harm.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people. Explain why, and suggest a better way.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give credit where it is due.
- Keep discussions on topic and in good faith.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or sexual content and attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Any other behavior a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commits) and anywhere someone
represents the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior troubles you, report it to the maintainer: use **Report content** on the
comment, issue or pull request and choose to report it to the repository's maintainers. Reports
are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared.

## What happens next

The maintainer reviews every report and may, depending on what happened, ask for a change, edit or
hide content, lock a discussion, or block a person from the project, temporarily or permanently.
The maintainer's decision is final.
