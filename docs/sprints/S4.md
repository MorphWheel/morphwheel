## Interpretations and additions (RD-11 to RD-14, approved with the sprint)

1. **RD-11, RF-13.** A slot with no target and no reason is not a hover: the hub keeps the files line, nothing
   highlights, a drop there cancels. More without plugins keeps its `NO_PLUGINS` reason (phase-3 §2.3).
2. **RD-12, DROPDESCRIPTION deferred.** M2 check 2 asked whether "Convert to JPG" can replace "Copy" next to
   the cursor. Qt gives the widget a `QMimeData`, not the `IDataObject`, so the text can only be set from a
   `platform`-layer `IDropTarget` wrapper around Qt's own (OLE keeps it in the `OleDropTargetInterface`
   window property), calling `IDataObject::SetData(CFSTR_DROPDESCRIPTION)` on DragEnter and DragOver, and
   proven with real drags. Not built in S4; the hub repeating the target (C5) covers v0.1.
3. **RD-13, the fading wheel keeps catching.** For 83 ms after a cancel and 250 ms after a drop the window is
   still up with its floor: a press there is swallowed and a drag re-entering gets "no drop". Accepted; the
   `WindowTransparentForInput` alternative re-creates the native window and needs a hand-tested drag (S5).
4. **RD-14, `--motion`.** Windows' animation switch is off over Remote Desktop, so without a way to force the
   animations the fades would never meet a real drag before M2. A development flag, not a setting.

Smaller choices, no decision needed: the icons for Compress, PDF, More and Cancel are text and glyphs until
S5 re-allows `qsvg` (phase-3 §7 wants Fluent icons); the English plurals are "1 file" / "%n files" so the
untranslated hub reads correctly; the accent is re-read at each show (a live accent change fires no scheme
signal); the shadow is 24 concentric rings, no blur effect.

## Plan review (two lenses) and what it changed

The plan was attacked before any code. **Drop safety and the drag loop:** no path accepts Move, falls
through inside the halo or accepts a stale target, because the three handlers are untouched and the
session is cleared at the first instant of every dismiss. It asked for, and got: the floor painted before
anything is computed; a dismiss token so an old fade can't hide a newer wheel; a real-time backstop; the
"dismissing" state derived from the animator; `windowOpacity()` asserted mid-fade; a way to run the fades
over RDP (RD-14). **Qt rendering and timing:** it measured the raster (3.4 ms direct, 0.5 ms with a layer)
and changed the design: one cached content layer faded as a group instead of per-primitive opacity (which
double-blends); everything after the floor clipped to the halo (a shadow or bulge could otherwise put alpha
outside it); injectable timers (orphan timers under a fake clock crash the suite, which then happened once
and was fixed with `qtbot.addWidget`); the last view frozen during the fade; hover keyed on
`highlight_slot`; "Segoe UI Variable" named first ("…Variable Text" resolves to Bold on Windows).

**Spike.** 1 818 frames scanned pixel by pixel (101 opacities × 3 scales × 3 DPRs × 2 image formats, with
antialiased fills, a scaled edge line and text over an alpha-1 floor): the worst alpha inside the halo was
1 and the outside always 0. Qt's premultiplied rounding never drops the floor.

## Findings

A code review (high effort) on the working tree found 7 items: the hub's "%n file(s)" (Qt shows the source
text literally without a translator: now "1 file" / "3 files"); no test for the floor under a painting

## The cleanup passes

Four lenses on the working tree. Applied: the animator owns the drop flash (per slot, like the bulge) and
the More arc, so the window keeps no animation state; `Accept.slot` carries the dropped slot (no region
recomputation); the window has no frame copy (the animator is the single source) and no content cache;
RF-13 lives where "hover" is defined, not in the hub text; the layer buffer is reused; the floor path and
the fonts are cached; `active()` allocates nothing; `_finish` is `reset()` plus the signal; the "High
Contrast wins" rule exists once; one fake clock, one two-plugin catalog and one session builder across the
suites; `slot_labels` / `item_labels` shared by the window and the tests. Skipped with reasons: a shared
High Contrast palette fixture (six lines, two suites), inlining `Motion.ms` (the tests use it), the
defensive guard in the fade handler (kept, labelled as such). The security review was not run: S4 adds no
I/O, subprocess, download, plugin or ctypes surface.

## UI pass

Every phase-3 §3 state was rendered to PNG offscreen and inspected in Light, Dark and High Contrast:
waiting (skeleton bars, plain hub), armed (labels, the file name and count), hover (accent, 4 px proud,
"→ JPG" / "3 files, 1 skipped"), disabled hover ("PNG is the source"), not supported (all dimmed,
"⊘ Not supported"), More open (the arc with its items, the hovered item lit), halo (the faint edge line,
"✕ Cancel"), the drop flash. One fix came out of it: the hub was tinted in the waiting state because the
pointer sits at the center; the tint now belongs to the Cancel state only. Zero durations with animations
off, High Contrast opaque with 2 px borders, every string through `tr()`, the wheel unmirrored (D3a).

## Proof posture

- **Proven offscreen:** the alpha floor in every state and animation frame; `hide()` after every fade,
  through the real timer and through the backstop; the S2 safety matrix; the motion table; High Contrast.
- **Reasoned from the construction:** the layered window's compositing of the alpha-1 floor (unchanged
  since S2, where real drags proved it); the clip keeping the shadow inside the halo (also tested).
- **Unverified until the hand tests:** a real drop during the appear animation (C28), nothing catching
  after the fade (C29), the live frame rate and the look on a real desktop (C30), High Contrast on a real
  theme and a live scheme switch (C31). Over RDP the animations are off unless `--motion on`.
- **Known and accepted:** the 83–250 ms dead zone (RD-13); `apply_size` raising while the wheel fades;
  three "access violation" messages at the teardown of `test_application.py`, present before S4 (the pool
  and the toasts; the tests pass), not isolated.
